Privacy Policy

Secretary (Lucina) · Last updated: 22 October 2026

Who we are

Secretary, with its assistant Lucina, is a personal assistant operated by Giuseppe Vitolo, available at getsecretary.tech. It is a private, single-user service: it is used only by its owner, Giuseppe Vitolo, and is not offered to the public. Contact: vitologiuseppe17@gmail.com.

What data is processed

The assistant processes the owner's own data that he chooses to give it, such as messages, reminders, notes, calendar entries and email. It does not collect data about other users, because there are none.

Google user data

Secretary connects, through Google OAuth, to the Gmail account mylucina20@gmail.com, which is the assistant's own mailbox. Access to Gmail (sending and reading mail) is used only to send and read the assistant's own mail when the owner asks for it.

  • OAuth tokens and any Google data are stored encrypted on the owner's own server.
  • Google user data is never sold, never shared with or transferred to third parties, and never used for advertising.
  • Google user data is never used to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.
  • No human other than the owner reads this data, except where required by law.

Secretary's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as the owner needs it for the assistant to work. You can revoke Secretary's access to a Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To ask for stored data to be deleted, email vitologiuseppe17@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
